# Contributor Intellectual-Property Policy

Effective for future external contributions only: no contribution may be
accepted until the contributor has signed a Free Eco Association
counsel-approved contributor license agreement or assignment agreement.

The final agreement must identify the contributor and contribution, confirm
the contributor's authority to contribute, grant the rights necessary for the
project's approved licensing model, and preserve notices and obligations for
any third-party material. It must also define how employee contributions,
contributor records, patent rights, and revocation requests are handled.

Maintainers must retain the signed agreement before merging a contribution and
must record the agreement reference in the review record. This policy does not
alter rights in contributions accepted before its effective date.
