# Free Eco Commercial License — Counsel Review Required

This is a release-control record, **not a license and not an offer of
commercial terms**. Do not distribute a paid product until qualified legal
counsel has supplied and approved a signed commercial license.

The approved agreement must, at minimum:

1. identify the licensor and only the Free Eco-owned deliverables covered;
2. grant access only while the customer is an active, fee-paid Association
   member or holds an active Business, Nonprofit, or Enterprise subscription
   or approved lump-sum entitlement;
3. prohibit copying, redistribution, sublicensing, credential sharing, and
   access outside the purchased entitlement, to the extent permitted by law;
4. define fees, renewals, taxes, suspension, termination, and post-termination
   access;
5. define support, updates, service availability, warranties, liability,
   indemnity, audit, privacy, data-processing, export-control, and governing
   law terms;
6. incorporate the eligibility policy and a fair notice/appeal process; and
7. expressly preserve all required upstream and third-party notices and
   licenses, including `LICENSE-APACHE` and `LICENSE-MIT`.

The agreement must not claim exclusive ownership of OpenFang-derived or other
third-party material and cannot retract open-source permissions already
granted to earlier recipients.
