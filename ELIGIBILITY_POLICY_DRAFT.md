# Free Eco Customer Eligibility Policy — Draft for Counsel Review

This policy is a proposed commercial eligibility standard. It is not yet an
enforceable customer term.

## Eligible customer categories

Access may be granted only to an applicant that has an active, paid
Association membership or an active paid Business, Nonprofit, Enterprise, or
approved lump-sum entitlement. Access is personal to the approved legal entity
and its authorized users.

## Environmental and ethical eligibility

Applicants must accurately represent their operations and meet the criteria
selected in the final commercial agreement. The Association should use
verifiable evidence rather than unsupported labels:

| Criterion | Acceptable evidence examples |
| --- | --- |
| Sustainable/ecological operations | A current published sustainability policy, relevant third-party certification, or documented measurable environmental program |
| Cruelty-free operations | Current certification or a documented policy and supply-chain evidence appropriate to the product or service |
| Vegan operations | Current certification or product/service and ingredient records supporting the claim |
| Organic operations | Current certification from the applicable jurisdiction, where an organic claim is made |

Counsel must define applicable jurisdictions, equivalencies, exceptions,
renewal periods, and whether every criterion or an approved subset is
required. The criteria must be applied consistently and in compliance with
applicable anti-discrimination, consumer-protection, competition, and
accessibility law.

## Review, records, and appeals

1. An authorized Association reviewer records the application, entitlement,
   evidence reviewed, decision, and decision date.
2. Approval expires with the payment entitlement or at the review date set by
   the final agreement.
3. The Association may request updated evidence on renewal or after a
   substantiated concern.
4. Before suspension or revocation, provide written notice, the reason, a
   reasonable correction period where appropriate, and an independent appeal
   route.
5. Retain only the minimum records needed for eligibility and entitlement
   administration, subject to the applicable privacy policy and retention law.
