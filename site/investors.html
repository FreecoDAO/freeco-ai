What is the use of FreEco.ai

It runs the work. Not a chat window you type into — a team of agents that keeps a company or a household going while you are asleep, and makes the ecological and health cost of each decision visible at the moment it is taken rather than in a report afterwards.

Runs your back office CRM, invoices, calls, projects and messages across 44 channels — on schedules, with spend caps and approval gates.
Makes the cost visible Every purchase, supplier and workflow carries an ecological and health cost. Agents surface it before you commit, not after.
Guardrails, not guidelines A cheaper supplier with a worse footprint is flagged, not quietly chosen. An agent cannot argue its way past a limit.
Healthy defaults for families A kid-safe role and health-aware defaults, because habits form early and most tools ignore that entirely.
Your data stays yours Self-hosted on hardware you own. It works with no internet at all if you want it to.
Honest about its own cost Running a model burns energy. Per-task energy and spend are shown, not hidden. A sustainability product that hides its footprint is not one.

Where this is going

Self-running sustainable AI teams

The shift is from assistants that answer to teams that operate. An eco-conscious company or nonprofit hires a structured set of agents — a CEO agent that plans and delegates, specialists that execute, gates where a human decides — and the organisation runs continuously inside its own sustainability limits.

An org chart, not a prompt CEO agent, developer, analyst, support, accountant. Each a signed agent with scoped permissions and its own budget.
Delegation with a human gate Plans become tasks, tasks become work, and anything consequential stops for approval that states the aim and the cost.
Sustainability is the constraint Teams optimise inside ecological limits rather than around them. That is the product, not a report bolted on afterwards.
Small organisations, real capacity A four-person nonprofit gets a back office it could never staff. That is the market, and it is largely unserved.
Why this is the commercial engine

Chat subscriptions sell seats. Running teams sell outcomes, and they run continuously, which makes revenue recurring by construction rather than by contract. Sustainability guardrails are the part competitors cannot add late: they have to be in the decision path from the beginning, and in this system they already are.

The category is already proving itself

Polsia reached roughly USD 10 m ARR in five months with 7,600 business customers, run by nine agents and zero employees, and raised USD 30 m at a USD 250 m valuation. That is the demand signal: companies will pay for agents that operate, not agents that answer.

FreEco.ai addresses the same shift with two things a closed platform cannot copy: the work stays on infrastructure the customer owns, and every action runs inside ecological and health guardrails rather than being measured after the fact.
