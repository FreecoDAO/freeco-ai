Why this exists

To help individuals, families and businesses live and trade more healthily and ecologically — by making the consequences of an everyday decision visible at the moment it is taken, not in a report afterwards.

Daily choicesEvery purchase, supplier and workflow carries an ecological and health cost. Agents surface it before you commit, not after.
Sustainable by constructionGuardrails an agent cannot argue its way past. A cheaper supplier with a worse footprint is flagged, not quietly chosen.
Families and kidsHealth-aware defaults and a kid-safe role, because habits are formed early and most tools ignore that entirely.
Honest about our own costRunning a model burns energy. The per-task energy and spend are shown rather than hidden — a sustainability product that hides its own footprint is not one.

Market: TAM · SAM · SOM

Areas are drawn in proportion to the values shown, so the picture matches the arithmetic rather than flattering it.

TAM — Total addressable market
Global AI software spend. Every organisation that could buy AI tooling.
SAM — Serviceable addressable market
Organisations needing self-hosted, private agents: regulated sectors, EU and Swiss data residency, public bodies, privacy-first SMEs.
SOM — Serviceable obtainable market
What we can realistically win in the first phase: Swiss and EU SMEs and associations already running open-source back offices.

Method, stated so it can be checked. TAM is the whole AI software market. SAM narrows to the AI agents segment — the fastest growing of the three at 49.6% CAGR — because an agent operating system sells into that segment, not into AI spend generally. The SOM figure is the vertical-AI market used as a proxy; our obtainable share is a fraction of it, being the part that requires self-hosting and EU or Swiss data residency. Refine with a bottom-up count of target organisations before quoting a SOM to an investor: a top-down proxy shows the shape, not the ceiling.
